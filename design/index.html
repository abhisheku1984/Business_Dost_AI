01Point of view

Premium does not mean complicated.

Business Dost AI is used between customers, in a busy shop, often one-handed. Every decision below removes a step, a question or a doubt — while still feeling considered and expensive.

Calm by default

One accent colour, generous space and no decoration that competes with the numbers. The interface never shouts.

Clarity over density

Each screen answers one question. Depth lives one tap away, never crammed onto the first view.

Built for real shops

Udhaar, UPI, GST and Hinglish are first-class citizens, not settings buried in an international template.

Voice is a primary input

Speaking is faster than typing in a busy shop. Voice mode gets its own calm surface, not a modal afterthought.

Trust is visible

Dost drafts, the owner decides. Confidence, reasoning and consequences are always shown before anything saves.

Light on the device

No heavy blur stacks, no hero video, 60 fps transitions. The premium feel comes from type, space and rhythm.

02Foundations

One system, quietly holding everything together.

Tokens are the contract. Colour, type, space, radius, elevation and icons are defined once and mirrored in tokens.json for implementation.

Speaking should feel like a conversation, not a command line.

06Motion

Motion that explains, not decorates.

Animations are short, interruptible and transform/opacity only. Every one has a purpose: confirm a tap, show where a sheet came from, or signal that Dost is listening.

Press confirmation

Scale 0.975 on tap, 160 ms. Applied to every button and card action.

--dur-fast · --ease-standard
Sheet entrance

Slides from the bottom edge with a fast start and soft landing.

--dur-slow · --ease-entrance
List item appear

Rows fade and rise 8 px, staggered 40 ms. Capped at 8 items per view.

--dur-base · --ease-entrance
Listening pulse

Two expanding rings, 3 s, ease-out. Pauses entirely when the app is backgrounded.

--dur-slower · --ease-standard

Reduced motion: rings become a static halo.

07Accessibility

Readable in a shop, in sunlight, one-handed.

Contrast, touch targets, dynamic type and screen-reader labels are treated as design materials — not a QA checklist at the end.

Touch & reach

Minimum 44 × 44 pt targets, 8 pt apart. Primary actions sit in the lower third of the screen.

44 × 44 minimum.
Visible icons may be smaller; the hit area never is. Row heights are at least 60 pt.
Keyboard focus ring 2–3 px evergreen ring with 2 px offset on light, 3 px on dark.
Dynamic type Reduce motion Screen readers VoiceOver

What we commit to

  • Text scales to 200% without clipping; cards grow vertically instead.
  • Never colour alone: every status has a label and a dot or icon.
  • One logical focus order; the Dost orb is reachable after tabs.
  • All icons decorative unless they carry the only label — then aria-label.
  • Voice flow has a full text alternative via the keyboard button.
  • Motion pauses when the app loses focus and respects the OS setting.

Dark mode is a first-class theme

Not an inversion. Surfaces are layered charcoal, borders do the work of shadows, and the evergreen brightens to mint for contrast on black.

Pure black is reserved for the device frame and splash; app surfaces lift to #121216 so cards stay legible in sunlight.

08Handoff

From review to production.

The tokens here are implementation-ready and mirrored in design/tokens.json. The same values map cleanly to Flutter or React Native.

Token contract

Semantic names, not hex codes, inside components. Theme switching becomes a single map swap.

/* design/tokens.css */
--brand: #0a6a56; /* evergreen */
--brand-text: #0a6a56; /* on light */
--surface: #ffffff;
--r-xl: 24px;
--dur-base: 240ms;

[data-theme="dark"] {
  --brand: #3ed3ad;
  --surface: #121216;
}
